Portugal / 2024

October 7–15, 2024 · Guided motorcycle tour

Portugal

A nine-day guided loop from Lisbon with Portugal Motorcycle Tours: up the Atlantic coast to Porto, north to the Minho and Peneda-Gerês, through the Douro Valley, south on the N2, and home across the Alentejo by Marvão and Évora.

Portugal Motorcycle Tours — North Tour

This loop was the guided North Tour from Lisbon: the Atlantic coast, a food tour in Porto, the Douro Valley, Trás-os-Montes, Peneda-Gerês and the N2 back south. Highly recommended if you want to ride Portugal with people who know every back road.

Day 1 · Mon, Oct 7

Lisbon up the Atlantic coast

Out of Lisbon and onto the back roads of the west coast: fishing and surfing villages, orchards, and the ocean alongside most of the way north to Nazaré and São Pedro de Moel.

Day 2 · Tue, Oct 8

Porto

A short coastal run into Porto and a food tour through the city’s small eateries.

By way of

  • Porto

Day 3 · Wed, Oct 9

Into the Douro Valley

East into the Douro, the world’s oldest demarcated wine region, following the river through terraced vineyards to Quinta de la Rosa above Pinhão, in the middle of the harvest.

By way of

  • Douro Valley
  • Pinhão

Day 4 · Thu, Oct 10

A day on the Douro

Off the bikes: out on the river by boat, along the Douro by train, and into the port lodges.

By way of

  • Pinhão
  • Douro River

Day 5 · Fri, Oct 11

North into the Minho

North out of the Douro through green-wine country toward the mountains of the Minho, by way of Casa do Penedo, the little house built into the boulders near Fafe.

By way of

  • Fafe
  • Casa do Penedo

Day 6 · Sat, Oct 12

Peneda-Gerês to Braga

Through Peneda-Gerês, Portugal’s only national park: granite ridges, oak and pine forest and stone villages, then down to Braga for the night.

By way of

  • Soajo
  • Peneda
  • Braga

Day 7 · Sun, Oct 13

South through the mountains

South through the mountains and gorges of the interior to Quinta Lusitania, a manor house near Santa Comba Dão, for the night.

Overnight

Quinta Lusitania

Santa Comba Dão, Portugal

Day 8 · Mon, Oct 14

Down the N2 to Marvão

South on the N2, Portugal’s longest road and its answer to Route 66, and on to the hilltop fortress of Marvão on the Spanish border.

By way of

  • N2
  • Marvão

Day 9 · Tue, Oct 15

The Alentejo and back to Lisbon

Out of Marvão across the cork-oak plains of the Alentejo by Elvas and Évora, and back to Lisbon to close the loop.

By way of

  • Marvão
  • Elvas
  • Évora
  • Lisbon
