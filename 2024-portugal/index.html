Portugal / 2024

Oct 7–15, 2024 · Motorcycle tour

Portugal

A nine-day guided loop from Lisbon with Portugal Motorcycle Tours: up the Atlantic coast to Porto, north to the Minho and Peneda-Gerês, through the Douro Valley, south on the N2, and home across the Alentejo by Marvão and Évora.

Portugal Motorcycle Tours — North Tour

This loop was the guided North Tour from Lisbon: the Atlantic coast, a food tour in Porto, the Douro Valley, Trás-os-Montes, Peneda-Gerês and the N2 back south. Highly recommended if you want to ride Portugal with people who know every back road.

Stage 1Stage 2Stage 3Stage 4Stage 5Stage 6

Stage 1

Lisbon up the Atlantic coast

Out of Lisbon by Sintra and onto the back roads of the west coast: fishing and surfing villages, fruit orchards, and the Atlantic road with the ocean alongside most of the way north.

By way of

  • Lisbon
  • Sintra
  • Nazaré
  • Aveiro

Stage 2

Porto

A short coastal run into Porto, then a day off the bike for a food tour through the city’s small eateries and tascas.

By way of

  • Porto

Stage 3

The Minho and Peneda-Gerês

North through the green-wine country of the Minho to Monção on the Spanish border, then into Peneda-Gerês, Portugal’s only national park: granite ridges, oak and pine forest, stone villages and mountain lagoons.

By way of

  • Braga
  • Monção
  • Peneda-Gerês National Park

Stage 4

The Douro Valley

East into the Douro, the world’s oldest demarcated wine region, following the river through terraced vineyards to Pinhão. One of the best riding days of the trip.

By way of

  • Vila Real
  • Pinhão

Stage 5

South on the N2

South through Viseu on the N2, Portugal’s longest road and its answer to Route 66, through the hill country of the interior.

By way of

  • Viseu
  • Serra da Estrela foothills

Stage 6

The Alentejo and back to Lisbon

Down to the hilltop fortress of Marvão on the Spanish border, across the cork-oak plains of the Alentejo to Évora, and back to Lisbon to close the loop.

By way of

  • Marvão
  • Évora
  • Lisbon
